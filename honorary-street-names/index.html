Fat Boys Avenue

●Schenck Avenue, between New Lots Avenue and Hegeman Avenue

East New York · 2025 enacted · 2026 dedication ceremony scheduled

Fat Boys Avenue honors The Fat Boys, the influential Brooklyn hip-hop trio made up of Mark “Prince Markie Dee” Morales, Damon “Kool Rock-Ski” Wimbley, and Darren “Buff Love” Robinson. Originally known as the Disco 3, the group emerged in the early 1980s and became known for its humor, beatboxing, hit records, and crossover visibility. The East New York block of Schenck Avenue between New Lots Avenue and Hegeman Avenue is being honorarily co-named Fat Boys Avenue in recognition of the group’s neighborhood roots and cultural legacy.

Official designation: Local Law 121 of 2025

How Honorary Street Co-Naming Works in NYC

An honorary street co-naming adds a commemorative sign while the legal street name and official address system remain unchanged.

  1. 1

    Community proposal

    Families, neighbors, and community organizations prepare a request for an honoree and a specific street corner, block, or street segment.

  2. 2

    Brooklyn Community Board 5 review

    Applicants submit a questionnaire, biography of the deceased honoree, and supporting neighbor signatures to CB5’s Transportation & TLC Committee.

  3. 3

    Council Member sponsorship

    After Community Board approval, the local Council Member may submit the request for City Council consideration.

  4. 4

    City law and DOT installation

    After City Council approval and the Mayor’s signature, NYC DOT installs the honorary street sign.

Honorary Co-Naming vs. Legal Street Renaming

Honorary Co-Naming

  • Adds a commemorative secondary sign
  • Original legal street name remains
  • Addresses and mail delivery remain unchanged
  • Used to honor local people, organizations, and community legacies

Legal Street Renaming

  • Replaces the official street name
  • May affect addresses, maps, records, and mail
  • Requires a broader legal and administrative process
  • Is less common than honorary co-naming
