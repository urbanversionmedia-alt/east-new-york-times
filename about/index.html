Who we are

The East New York Times (ENYT) is an independent community news publication based in East New York, Brooklyn. We report on the decisions, people and services that shape daily life in East New York, Cypress Hills, City Line, Starrett City and Brownsville.

The East New York Times is not affiliated with The New York Times.

We are privately owned. No newspaper chain, publicly traded company, hedge fund, private equity firm, political organization or religious institution owns any part of this publication or has any say in what we cover.

What we cover

  • Brooklyn Community Board 5 meetings, votes and committees (CB5 meeting schedule)
  • Housing, homeownership, tenants and NYCHA
  • Development, rezoning and transit
  • Schools, youth programs and public safety
  • Benefits access, including Medicaid, SNAP and city programs
  • Local elections and the officials who represent East New York
  • Neighborhood life, culture and community events

Who runs ENYT

Deric Johnson is the publisher and editor of The East New York Times and reports most of its stories, photos and video. He is responsible for everything the publication publishes.

ENYT is published by Urban Version Media LLC, a Brooklyn multimedia production company co-founded by Deric Johnson and Ro Johnson. Urban Version Media's client work never includes coverage in The East New York Times. Our editorial standards explain how we keep the two separate.

How we report

Coverage in The East New York Times cannot be bought, traded or promised. We report from public meetings, records, interviews and our own photography and video, and we correct our mistakes in the open.
