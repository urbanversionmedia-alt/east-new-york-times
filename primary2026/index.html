CB5 Brooklyn General Board Meeting — Last Chance Before the Summer Break

The CB5 Brooklyn General Board meeting is Wednesday, June 24 at 6:30 PM at 127 Pennsylvania Avenue. This is the last meeting before the board goes on summer recess until September.

Deric Johnson · Reporter, East New York Times · June 23, 2026 · 3 min read
Wednesday, June 24 — Last Meeting Until September
CB5 Brooklyn General Board Meeting
Wednesday, June 24, 2026 at 6:30 PM
127 Pennsylvania Avenue, 3rd Floor, Brooklyn NY 11207
Open to all residents

Community Board 5 Brooklyn holds its last General Board meeting of the season on Wednesday, June 24 at 6:30 PM at 127 Pennsylvania Avenue. After this meeting, the board goes on summer recess and does not meet again until September 2026.

The General Board meeting is open to all residents. Public comment is on the agenda. You do not need to be a CB5 member to speak.

Three Issues Worth Raising at the Meeting

  • Innovative Urban Village grocery store The development at Flatlands and Louisiana promised a 17,000 sq ft fresh food grocery store in Phase 1A. Is it leased? When does it open? East New York is a food desert and this commitment needs a timeline.
  • Broadway Junction construction timeline MTA and NYCEDC are spending $495M at Broadway Junction. What are the short-term safety fixes for residents while the full project runs through 2031?
  • Essential Plan coverage cliff The August 30 deadline is approaching. Has CB5 reached out to local health providers to ensure residents get notified in time?

How to Participate

Show up at 127 Pennsylvania Ave, 3rd Floor by 6:30 PM. Bring your address and a specific concern. Public comment typically opens within the first 30 minutes. The meeting runs until 8:30 PM.
