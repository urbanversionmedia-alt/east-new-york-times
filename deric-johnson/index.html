About Deric

Deric Johnson is the publisher and editor of The East New York Times. He reports on Brooklyn Community Board 5, housing and homeownership, NYCHA, local government, elections, public safety and neighborhood life in East New York and Cypress Hills, and he shoots much of the publication's photography and video.

He co-founded Urban Version Media, the Brooklyn production company that publishes ENYT. Its client work never includes ENYT coverage.

Deric is a retired member of the New York City Police Department after 22 years of service. ENYT discloses that history so readers can weigh its public-safety reporting with full information. Read more in our editorial standards.
